# Gameplay Guide

## Start
1. Open the game, type a **detective name** (max 20 characters) and press **START INVESTIGATION**. A short intro plays (press *Skip intro* any time).
2. Your **dashboard** shows rank, XP, progress, mission cards and achievements.

## Missions and unlocking
Mission 1 is open. Completing a mission unlocks the next. After Mission 5 the **Final mystery** unlocks. Completed missions can be replayed with fresh data; the best score is kept.

## Solving puzzles
* **Conveyor levels:** items fall down the belt. Click/tap a bin (or press keys **1–9**) to route the lowest item. Right = combo and the belt speeds up; wrong or missed = lose a System Integrity shield. The ticker under the belt explains every decision. Enable **Relaxed mode** for no timer.
* **Physics and slider levels:** slide the pivot until the beam balances (mean); slide the divider until both crowds are equal (median); turn the r-dial until your cloud matches the target; slide the axis/k and watch the meter or curves.
* **Labs:** experiment freely (chart type, missing-value tool, model inputs); the lab shows the consequence, then you commit (Publish / Deploy / Lock in).
* **Cleaning datasets:** click rows to flag duplicates; click empty cells to flag missing values; type into cells like a spreadsheet; tick rows and press *Remove selected rows*. Use the paper-archive notes and column statistics shown. Mission 2's last task allows 3 submissions and scores by accuracy.
* **Charts:** click bars/points directly (or use the table alternative). Read axes first.
* **Prediction rule:** choose a feature, slide the threshold, watch accuracy; lock in when you reach the target.
* **Audit levels:** tap flawed claims in the report; a sound claim tapped by mistake costs XP.

## Hints, scoring, achievements
Hint −20 XP (up to 2 per puzzle). Wrong attempt −10. Puzzle +100, practical +150, +25 for no hint, +100 mission completion. Run score never drops below 0. Your total XP is the sum of your *best* mission scores, so replaying cannot inflate XP.

Badges: First Clue, Clean Data, Pattern Master, Visualization Expert, Prediction Pro, Perfect Investigation, Master Detective.

## Saving, resuming, resetting
Progress saves automatically in your browser. **Continue investigation** resumes where you left. *Settings → Reset* deletes everything after confirmation. You can change your name in Settings without losing progress.

## Troubleshooting
* Charts look plainer than expected → Chart.js could not load (offline); the built-in renderer is used and the game still works.
* Progress missing → private browsing/cleared site data/different browser.
* Sound silent → it is off by default; enable in Settings.
